How it works

Four steps, entirely in your browser

1

Upload your file

Drag and drop a .docx, .xlsx, .xls, or .csv — or paste text into the rich text editor. Nothing leaves your machine.

2

Extract content

Mammoth.js parses Word XML; SheetJS parses spreadsheet rows and columns into structured data.

3

Run 15 rules

The conversion pipeline normalises headings, phone links, tables, symbols, bold, underline, and more — in sequence.

4

Copy or download

Preview the result, copy the HTML to clipboard, or download as a .txt file ready for your CMS.
